Fixed Sky

Earth spins and goes around the Sun. The stars barely move because they are so far away. The axis keeps pointing the same way — so south stays south.

From space

The gold line is Earth's axis. It stays aimed at the same point in the sky all year. That is why the North Star stays north and the Southern Cross stays south.
