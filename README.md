# Fixed Sky

Interactive 3D explainer for a simple question:

> If Earth goes around the Sun, why is the Southern Cross still in the south? Why doesn’t the night sky flip?

**Open `index.html` or the live Vercel URL.** No build step. Three.js from a CDN.

## What it shows

- Earth spinning on a **23.44° tilt**
- Earth orbiting the Sun
- A **fixed** star field — Polaris north, Southern Cross south, all year
- Orion vs Scorpius switching which one is up at midnight (the Sun hides the other half)
- A “cheat” button that drags the stars in close so you can *see* orbital parallax — then reminds you real stars are ~270,000× farther than that fake shell

## Views

- **Space** — watch the whole machine
- **Night sky · south** — camera on Victoria, looking out
- **Night sky · north** — camera in the northern mid-latitudes, Polaris almost still

Drag to orbit in space view. Month slider jumps the year. Pause / speed as needed.
